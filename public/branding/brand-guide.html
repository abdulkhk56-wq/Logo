01 · Symbol

A real gas flame, drawn in three flat layers

The symbol is a burning gas flame: a tall main tongue and a shorter second tongue, as a real flame moves. Depth comes from three solid layers, never from gradients, so it prints, embroiders and engraves cleanly.

  • Flame

    Oil, gas and the energy of the plant, shown plainly. Engineers, operators and executives recognise it immediately.

  • Layers

    Each layer sits inside the one behind it, the way a real flame burns hotter towards its core. They also stand for SAFI's job: removing noise layer by layer until only what matters remains.

  • Stable base

    The base is broad and settled, and the flame rises straight up. It is controlled, not wild: a process running inside its limits.

  • One colour

    In a single colour, the core is cut out of the outer flame. The symbol stays a flame in black, white or navy.

04 · Colour

Navy for the company. Three flame colours for the symbol.

Inside operator displays (ISA-101), orange is commonly an alarm colour. On control-room screens, the flame colours appear only in the logo. Interface states use their own alarm palette, never the brand colours.

05 · Typography

Archivo, the typeface behind the wordmark

Archivo is a grotesk with a width axis, built for both print and screens. It is open source (SIL OFL), so it can be used in software, documents and print without licensing friction. Use the wider cuts for headlines and the narrower ones for data.

06 · Usage

Clear space, size and misuse

Clear space

Half the flame width on every side.

Minimum size

Lockup ≥ 96px / 25mm wide
Symbol ≥ 16px / 5mm tall

Below 96px wide, use the symbol on its own.

Backgrounds

Primary on white or Mist. Reversed on Navy. On photographs or any other colour, use one-colour white or black.
