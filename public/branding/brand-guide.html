01 · Idea

The name already says what the product does

In Arabic, صافي means pure, clear: what remains after filtering. SAFI takes a flood of alarms and process noise and leaves the operator with a clear picture. The identity is built on the name itself, not on a picture.

صافي
pureclearfilterednet
  • Symbol

    The letter ص (Sad), the first letter of the name, reversed out of a navy tile. A single letter, recognisable in both the Arab world and abroad, and robust at any size.

  • Two scripts

    The name always appears in Arabic and English, set from one type family so both scripts share stroke weight and rhythm. That is how established regional energy companies present themselves.

  • Gold rule

    A thin gold line separates the scripts: a precise, measured detail, like a scale mark on an instrument.

  • No pictures

    No flames, drops or circuits. The mark relies on letterforms and proportion, so it doesn't date and can't be mistaken for an icon.

04 · Colour

Navy carries the brand. Gold marks the detail.

Solid colours only. Navy and white do almost all the work. Gold appears only as thin rules and small highlights, never as a large fill or as text.

In operator displays (ISA-101), colour is reserved for abnormal conditions. Navy, white and grey suit a calm screen, and the brand never competes with alarm colours.

05 · Typography

Readex Pro, one family for both scripts

Readex Pro covers Arabic and Latin with matched weights. It is the family the logo is drawn from, and it is open source (SIL OFL) for software, documents and print.

06 · Usage

Clear space, size and misuse

Clear space

Half the tile width on every side.

Minimum size

Bilingual logo ≥ 140px / 35mm wide
Latin or Arabic logo ≥ 90px / 22mm
Symbol ≥ 16px / 5mm

Backgrounds

Primary on white or Mist. Reversed on Navy. On photographs or other colours, use the one-colour versions.
