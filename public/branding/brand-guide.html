01 · Symbol

Three plates. The flame exists where they agree.

The symbol is not a drawing of a flame. It is a flame built with compass and straight-edge, then read through three equal plates. Only the outline that survives the plates is kept.

  • Plates

    Filter elements, heat-exchanger plates, the level bars on an operator display. Equal width, equal gaps, one base line: control and stability.

  • Gaps

    What is taken out. SAFI filters noise and nuisance alarms so that only what matters reaches the operator.

  • Flare core

    The centre plate carries the main tongue and is the only coloured part. Energy sits at the centre, and everything around it is held steady.

  • Geometry

    Grid module 6. Plates 24, gaps 12. Six construction circles, and the tips fall exactly on the plate edges. Every edge in the file is a circular arc or a straight line.

02 · Logo

Versions

The wordmark is drawn, not typeset. Strokes and terminals are vertical or horizontal like the plates, and the letters sit on the symbol's base line. Cap height is 0.52 × symbol height, and the gap between symbol and word is one plate width.

04 · Colour

SAFI Navy carries the brand. Flare is the core.

Solid colours only. Navy does most of the work. Flare appears on the centre plate and, at most, one highlight per layout.

Inside operator displays (ISA-101), orange is commonly an alarm colour. On control-room screens, Flare appears only in the logo. Interface states use their own alarm palette, never the brand colours.

05 · Typography

Barlow, for everything that isn't the logo

Barlow is a grotesk with slightly rounded, squared forms that sit naturally beside the wordmark. It is open source (SIL OFL), so it can be used in software, documents and print without licensing friction. Never use it to retype the logo.

06 · Usage

Clear space, size and misuse

Clear space

One plate width on every side: 24 of the symbol's 96 units.

Minimum size

Lockup ≥ 100px / 25mm wide
Symbol ≥ 16px / 5mm tall
Small-size cut ≤ 24px

Below 100px wide, use the symbol on its own.

Backgrounds

Primary on white or Mist. Reversed on Navy or Deep Navy. On photographs or any other colour, use one-colour white or black.
